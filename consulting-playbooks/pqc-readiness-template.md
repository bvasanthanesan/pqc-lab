\# PQC Readiness Assessment Template



This template helps organizations evaluate their preparedness for Post‑Quantum Cryptography migration.



\## 1. Current Cryptographic Inventory

\- Algorithms in use (RSA, ECC, AES, etc.)

\- Key lengths and protocols



\## 2. Risk Assessment

\- Quantum vulnerability of current algorithms

\- Business impact if compromised



\## 3. Migration Strategy

\- Identify critical systems for PQC upgrade

\- Plan hybrid deployments (classical + PQC)



\## 4. Roadmap

\- Short‑term: CBOM creation, hybrid TLS/IPsec pilots

\- Medium‑term: PKI migration, crypto‑agility framework

\- Long‑term: Full PQC adoption aligned with NIST standards
